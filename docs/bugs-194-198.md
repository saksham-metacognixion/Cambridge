## Why the hero edges faded

The banners with a client file (16 Oct folder `docs/client-assets/website-images-oct16`) were cut by
`tools/banners/composite.py extract`, which copies the **old live cut-out's alpha** (Bug 175: clean edges). On five banners the
old live file stopped short of the picture on the start side (table, couch, bed, coat cut by its left edge) and feathered that
edge over up to ~100 live px; that fade and that crop came through, and three of them also had `fade: "start"` (CSS mask) in
`banner-art.json`.

New mode `extract_full` (`composites.json "full": true`): the old silhouette is kept where it is clean, the client file's own
key is used in the strip the old file cropped or feathered (start side + under its bottom), at the client file's own scale, and
the pill spot is read straight off the client canvas (no fit). Pill strokes seen through a translucent edge and the old file's
faint feather pixels are removed. `fade` / `bleed` flags of the re-cut banners are dropped.
