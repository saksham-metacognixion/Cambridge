How `restore` works: inside the wide feathers only (≥ 7 px of partial alpha; the 1–2 px anti-aliased outline is untouched) alpha is
divided by 0.15, so the picture under the brush comes back opaque and the edge ends where the brush ran out. Two guards keep the old
photo's backdrop from coming back with it (the Figma `-bg` layer under the box for Figma crops, and the feather's faintest pixels
when they are light), and restored islands not joined to the people are dropped. The sources are the pipeline's own (live files,
Figma layers, 14 Oct originals), so `python3 -I tools/banners/prepare.py --unfade-only` reproduces every file.
