## Method
`tools/banners/client_cutout.py` (Bugs 215-217) re-cuts each banner from its client file: it keys only background connected
to the outside, takes the pill strokes out of the key, and reads `w / h / pills / end / drop 0` off the canvas. The tickets'
edge defects (pill dots on the outline, notches at every pill row, straight vertical cuts, stray specks) came from the
130b85c cut-outs. The re-cut does not produce them. Two changes to the extractor:
- **Enclosed gaps with pill strokes** (Bugs 222/223): a gap between two people that is shaded a little (mean diff 4.4-4.5) was
  kept opaque, pill lines and all. With stroke evidence (>= 40 px) and >= 2000 px it is background up to diff 6. Real gaps
  measured 9.9k-16k px; pockets inside the ventilator and the coat logos are <= 360 px and stay opaque.
- **`POCKET_MIN` 20 -> 150** (Bug 230): 20-100 px "pockets" were white logo letters and pale specks on scrubs (a hole in the
  TBI wordmark). Real gaps measured >= 229 px.
Re-run on every file: only the intended pixels changed, and every `w / h / pills / end` stayed identical (KSA LTAC, Paediatric
Post-Acute, RTA, Spinal Cord, TBI and Testimonials reproduce the 215-217 entries exactly).

KSA Our Care hijab: `tools/region-photos/recolour_hijab_pil.py` (pure-Pillow port of `recolour-hijab.py`, same NAVY and shading
rule) on the whole 2x cut.
