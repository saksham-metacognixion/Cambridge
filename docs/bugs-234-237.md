## Root cause
- Find a Doctor and Geriatric (979090f) were cut by `client_cutout.py`; Homecare and ICU (130b85c) by the older pipeline. Both
  key by a colour threshold. The client's figures have a soft 2-3 px edge, so the threshold line wanders with pixel noise
  (staircase / jagged outlines). Where a pill stroke meets a figure the flood follows stroke-coloured edge pixels into the cloth
  (bites / notches through which the line shows). The soft edge pixels keep the pale backdrop or stroke colour in them (light or
  green fringe on the dark pill shapes and strokes). White cloth on the pale gradient gave nicks and stray bits.
- The **straight segments** of the abaya below the tablet (Bug 234) are in the client's own file: the client cut the abaya there
  as a polyline (kinks of 4-6 degrees). The old cut-out reproduced it exactly, with a pale line and green pill pixels along it.

## Method: `tools/banners/rematte.cjs` (new) + `tools/banners/rematte-234-237.json`
Each cut-out is re-made from its client file **onto its existing pixel grid**: same size, same scale and same spot. The grid was
registered against the client file (mean difference 0.8-1.6 / 255; scale within 0.5 %), so `src/data/banner-art.json` is
untouched and scale, position and pills stay where they were.
1. Hard key: `client_cutout.py` as committed in 979090f, with the figure mask saved before its blur (one added line after
   `fg = fg.point(...)` stroke-stub step: `Image.new('L', (W, H), 0)` pasted with `fg` at `(RX0, RY0)`, saved as `<out>_hard.png`).
   Find a Doctor: run on the composite trimmed to 1365 rows (the client file has 5 backdrop rows under the people).
2. Pill-strip closing (radius 10) of the key only next to strokes, so stroke bites are re-examined, not kept.
3. Matting in a 5 px band: alpha = projection of the pixel on the backdrop -> cloth line, colour un-blended from the backdrop
   (removes the fringe). Backdrop = the gradient model, or a local stroke-aware sample only where the pill mask says a stroke can
   be behind. Where cloth and backdrop are alike (white coat, grey sweater) the key decides, with level-set smoothing
   (sigma 2 px, re-thresholded: a straight outline with a ~1 px sharp edge, not a blur).
4. Genuine gaps that the key kept opaque are seeded as `pockets` (ICU: between stethoscope tube and neck; Geriatric: between the
   man's sleeve and the nurse; KSA Homecare: between the abaya and the wheelchair arm).
5. Bug 234 only: `edgeZones`: the abaya outline below the tablet (rows 672-829) is smoothed along its length (Gaussian, sigma 20
   composite rows): each row's edge profile is shifted by at most 1.08 composite px (0.8 cut-out px). The edge keeps its own
   softness.
6. Specks off the figure are dropped; resampling is premultiplied Lanczos-3; output islands < 20 px cleared; lossless WebP.
No blur, CSS clipping, feathering or offsets are used to hide defects.
