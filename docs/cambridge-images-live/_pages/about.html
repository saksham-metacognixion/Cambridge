Cambridge Hospital

A Leader in Post-Acute and Rehabilitation Care

We are a specialized hospital for rehabilitation and long-term care in the GCC region

At Cambridge Hospital, we provide comprehensive medical care and advanced rehabilitation services for patients requiring short-term recovery as well as long-term support for chronic conditions and complex injuries.

We deliver personalized care tailored to each patient’s needs, focusing on recovery, independence, and improved quality of life. We also encourage active involvement of family members as an integral part of the care journey.

Journey of Excellence

2012

Group Established

The holding company was incorporated with a mandate to launch LTC facilities in the region.

2014

Abu Dhabi & Al Ain Facility

Phase I in Abu Dhabi launched in June 2014, Phase II January 2015, and Al Ain – April 2015.

2015

Launch of TVU Program

Launched the first Transitional Vent Unit (TVU) program for mechanically ventilated patients.

2016

UAE & KSA Expansion

Cambridge Hospital secured its SAGIA license, and Al Ain Facility Phase II became operational.

2019

Dhahran Facility Launch

Launched first dedicated inpatient and outpatient rehabilitation facility in Eastern Province.

2020

Abu Dhabi Expansion

Expanded Abu Dhabi facility capacity is expected to eventually reach 106 beds.

2021

Group Acquired by Amanat

Strategic acquisition by Amanat Holdings drives accelerated sustainable growth.

2023

IECC Jeddah (100 Beds)

Group acquired majority stake in IECC Jeddah to drive its continued regional growth.

2023

Al Ain Expansion

Expanded Al Ain facility capacity to reach 109 beds following the latest expansion.

2024

Al Mudeef Center Abu Dhabi

Secured PPP to manage Zayed Higher Organization’s Mudeef facility.

2024

Al Khobar Facility

Commissioned one of the largest dedicated long-term care and ICU facilities in Eastern Province.

2025

Al Khobar Expansion

Khobar licensed 100 more beds, including 30 for rehabilitation, for a total of 200 beds.

2025

Cambridge Health Group Launch

Formed as the regional healthcare holding platform to drive strategic growth and portfolio expansion.

2026

Khobar Facility Expansion

We increased the Khobar facility’s capacity to 150 beds as part of our Saudi Arabia expansion.

2026

Full Ownership – Amanat

Amanat’s full ownership of Cambridge Health Group supports growth and integration.

2026

Identity Unification

CMRC UAE, KSA, IECC, and Al Mudeef unite under one brand, Cambridge Hospital.

2026

Home Care Launch – KSA

We soft-launched Cambridge Home Care in Saudi Arabia, with initial patient services.
