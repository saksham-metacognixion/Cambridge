Dr. Majed Osaylan

General Practitioner

Sub Specialties

Post-Stroke & Post-Injury Care
Post-Surgical Recovery
Preventive Care
Monitoring & Complication Prevention

Professional Summary

Dr. Majed Taha Majed Osaylan is a General Practitioner in the Extended Care Department at Cambridge Hospital, providing comprehensive, patient-centered care for individuals in long-term and extended care. This role covers the day-to-day management of medically stable and chronically ill patients — including those recovering from stroke, injury, or major surgery — with a focus on close monitoring, prevention of complications, and continuity of care in coordination with the multidisciplinary rehabilitation team.
