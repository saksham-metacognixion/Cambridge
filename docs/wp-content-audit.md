# WordPress content audit (6 Oct 2026)

Source: `docs/cambridgehospital.WordPress.2026-10-06.xml` (the live site's export: 64 pages = 32 English + 32 Arabic, 258 news posts, menus, footer and Media Hub content blocks, 758 attachment URLs). Method: every text fragment (headings, paragraphs, list items, link labels) of each live page was checked against the built page it maps to (`npm run build`), per language. "Missing" = not present word for word on the built page; the reason column says why it was left out. Rule applied (the brief, rule 5): the export fills what was missing; English copy already taken from Figma or from the client's 5 Oct documents was not replaced by the older live wording.

What the export does NOT contain: doctor, hospital, insurer, testimonial, leadership and condition records (only the post-type definitions), the forms (a plugin shortcode), the legal page texts (all six are empty on the live site too), and the image files themselves (URLs only; downloads are blocked by a Cloudflare bot check, NW1 / WP9).
